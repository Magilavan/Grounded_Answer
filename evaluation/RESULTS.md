### Q01: What is the maximum amount of countable resources a household can have and still be eligible?

*Decision: ANSWER*

```
A household is not eligible where the total countable resources of the household exceed $4,000. [§2.4.1]

Resources held jointly with a person who is not a household member are countable in proportion to the recipient's beneficial interest. Where the proportion cannot be established, an equal division is presumed. [§2.4.3]

The following are not countable resources —
(a) the home in which the household ordinarily resides;
(b) one motor vehicle per household;
(c) household goods and personal effects;
(d) funds held in a designated education savings account for a dependent child;
(e) the surrender value of a life insurance policy where that value does not exceed $1,500. [§2.4.2]

A household is not eligible where countable income exceeds the applicable threshold. The thresholds are —
| Household size | Monthly threshold |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | $1,180 |
| 2 | $1,590 |
| 3 | $2,000 |
| 4 | $2,410 |
| 5 | $2,820 |
| each additional member | + $410 | [§6.6.1]
```

### Q03: What is the definition of a dependent child under the manual?

*Decision: ANSWER*

```
Dependent child — a person under the age of 18 who is a household member, or a person aged 18 to 20 who is a household member and is enrolled in secondary education. [§1.4.5]

The needs figure is increased by $140 per month where the household includes a dependent child under the age of 2. [§7.3.2]

A sanction must not be imposed where the household includes a dependent child under the age of 2, or where the recipient has been assessed as requiring assistance with two or more activities of daily living. [§10.5.3]

A person aged 16 or 17 may be eligible where that person —
(a) is not a household member of any other household; and
(b) has no person with parental responsibility able and willing to provide support; or
(c) is a parent of a dependent child residing with them. [§2.3.1]
```
